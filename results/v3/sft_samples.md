### If a train travels 60 km in 2 hours, what is its average speed?

> Let's denote the speed of the train as \( v \) km/h.
> 
> The train travels 60 km in 2 hours, so its speed is \( v = 60 \times 2 = 120 \text{ km/h} \) km/h.
> 
> The average speed of the train is \( v = \frac{120}{60} \).
> 
> So, the average speed of the train is \( \frac{120}{60} \).
> 
> The answer is: \frac{120}{60} \approx 1.25.
