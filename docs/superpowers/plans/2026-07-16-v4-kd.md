## Task 1 — `kd_loss` (core, pure, TDD)
`src/tinylm/kd.py::kd_loss(student_logits, teacher_topk_vals, teacher_topk_idx,
hard_targets, *, alpha, temperature, ignore_index=-100, chunk_size=4096)`.
- Top-k forward-KL: gather student logits at teacher's top-k indices; softmax both at
  temperature T over the k; `KL = Σ p_t (log p_t − log q_s)`; scale by T².
- Mix: `(1−alpha)·chunked_cross_entropy(...) + alpha·T²·KL`, chunked over N=B·T.
- Tests (`tests/test_kd.py`): student==teacher ⇒ KL 0; alpha=0 ⇒ == chunked_cross_entropy;
  alpha=1 ⇒ pure scaled-KL; top-k gather correctness; T² scaling; ignore_index on CE part;
  fully-masked batch ⇒ finite.
- Commit: `feat(v4): top-k forward-KL + CE knowledge-distillation loss`.
